# Contributing

The contribution workflow is in the [Contributing section of the README](README.md#contributing).

In short: source every number in `docs/SOURCES.md`, use public domain material only, record any decision in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and name it in your commits, and rebuild `index.html` before you commit. If you work with an AI assistant, it follows [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md); your prompts stay on your machine and only the reasoning is published.
